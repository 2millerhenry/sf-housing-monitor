How to get an app password
  • Gmail Turn on 2-Step Verification, then create an app password under Google Account › Security.
  • iCloud Turn on two-factor authentication, then generate an app-specific password in your Apple Account.
  • Fastmail, Yahoo, AOL Create an app password in the account security section.
  • Outlook.com, Hotmail Not supported. Microsoft no longer allows app passwords for mail.
